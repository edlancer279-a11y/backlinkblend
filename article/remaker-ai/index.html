What Is Remaker AI? Features, Face Swap & Pricing Guide

A comprehensive 2026 guide to Remaker AI — exploring its AI face swap tool, text-to-image generator, photo enhancer, credit pricing models, and safety standards.

Evelyn Vance
Executive Editor, Technology & AI
Published 2026-09-16
6 min read • 8 min audio
Editorial illustration demonstrating Remaker AI synthetic face swap interface, neural image upscaling, and creative editing features.

Remaker AI is a web-based artificial intelligence content creation platform designed for image editing, synthetic media generation, and automated photo manipulation. Most famous for its single-photo, multi-photo, and video-based face swapping tools, Remaker AI enables content creators, digital marketers, and casual users to create realistic visual edits without requiring high-end graphic design software or complex machine learning expertise.

As synthetic digital face swapping technology evolves across digital media, understanding how platforms like Remaker AI balance creative flexibility with user accessibility becomes essential. For creators interested in mastering prompt creation for AI visual tools, see our complete AI image generator prompts guide.

Core Features of Remaker AI

Remaker AI provides a modular toolkit of creative visual tools accessible directly through any modern web browser:

  • AI Face Swap (Photo & Video): Allows users to swap faces seamlessly across single headshots, group photos, and video clips with automated skin tone and lighting matching.
  • Text-to-Image Generator: Converts descriptive text prompts into synthetic digital artwork, avatar styles, and photorealistic images.
  • Image Upscaler & Enhancer: Automatically increases image resolution and sharpness while removing digital noise from low-resolution photographs.
  • Background Remover & Object Eraser: Isolates subjects and deletes unwanted background elements using intelligent semantic masking.

Remaker AI Credit System & Pricing Breakdown

Unlike subscription-heavy design platforms, Remaker AI operates primarily on a flexible pay-as-you-go credit system:

Plan / Tier Credit Package Best Suited For
Free Trial Credits Limited complimentary credits upon new account signup. First-time users testing platform tools.
Starter Package ($5.99) 200 credits with no monthly expiration date. Casual creators and social media enthusiasts.
Pro Package ($19.99) 1,000 credits for high-resolution photo & video processing. Freelancers and digital marketers.
Enterprise Credit Bulk Scalable credit bundles up to 20,000 credits for heavy API usage. Agencies and software developers.

How to Perform a Face Swap on Remaker AI

Executing a face swap on Remaker AI involves three straightforward steps:

  1. Upload the Original Target Image: Select the base photo or video where you want the new face to appear.
  2. Upload the Source Face Photo: Choose a clear, well-lit portrait photo containing the target face you wish to transfer.
  3. Generate & Download: Click "Swap Face" and wait a few seconds while the neural network aligns features, skin tones, and shadows before downloading the final output.

Safety & Ethical Considerations

Remaker AI enforces content safety policies prohibiting non-consensual face swapping, explicit adult content generation, and deceptive impersonation of public figures. Users must ensure they hold rights to all uploaded portrait assets.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is Remaker AI free to use?

Remaker AI provides free trial credits upon account creation. After trial credits are exhausted, users can purchase pay-as-you-go credit packages starting at $5.99.

Do Remaker AI credits expire?

No. Unlike monthly subscriptions, purchased credits on Remaker AI generally remain active in your account balance until used.

Does Remaker AI support multi-face swapping in group photos?

Yes. Remaker AI features a dedicated multi-face swap tool that detects multiple faces in a single group photograph and allows users to swap each individual face independently.

Can I use Remaker AI on mobile devices?

Yes. Remaker AI is accessible through mobile web browsers on iOS and Android devices without requiring a mobile app installation.
