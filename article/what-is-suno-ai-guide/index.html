What Is Suno AI? Features, Song Generator & Pricing Guide

A comprehensive 2026 guide to Suno AI — exploring its text-to-music generator, v3.5 neural audio models, custom lyrics creator, commercial licensing, and pricing.

Evelyn Vance
Executive Editor, Technology & AI
Published 2026-09-17
6 min read • 8 min audio
Editorial illustration demonstrating Suno AI digital audio workstation interface, neural music generation, and audio spectrum controls.

Suno AI is a groundbreaking generative artificial intelligence music platform designed to create full studio-quality songs—complete with realistic singing voices, rich instrumentation, dynamic arrangement, and professional audio mastering—from simple natural language text descriptions.

As generative audio synthesis technology rapidly transforms media production, tools like Suno AI empower musicians, content creators, game developers, and marketers to compose original music across any genre in seconds. For creators building multimedia visual workflows alongside custom soundtracks, explore our detailed Viggle AI video animation guide.

Key Features of Suno AI

Suno AI offers a comprehensive set of music generation capabilities accessible via its intuitive web platform and mobile apps:

  • Text-to-Song Generation: Describe a musical style, mood, or subject (e.g., "an upbeat synthwave track with energetic drums and soaring vocals about space exploration") to produce two complete songs within seconds.
  • Custom Lyrics Mode: Input your own custom poetry or song lyrics while specifying acoustic genres, vocal gender, tempo, and song structure.
  • Suno v3.5 Audio Engine: Powered by state-of-the-art neural audio models capable of generating songs up to 4 minutes long with pristine acoustic fidelity and natural verse-chorus transitions.
  • Audio Inpainting & Stem Separation: Extend existing tracks, modify specific sections, or download separated instrumental and vocal audio stems for professional DAW editing.

Suno AI Pricing & Plan Comparison

Suno AI provides flexible plan options ranging from free daily exploration credits to commercial creator tiers:

Plan / Tier Monthly Credits & Songs Commercial Rights
Basic Plan (Free) 50 daily credits (up to 10 songs per day). Non-commercial personal use only.
Pro Plan ($10/mo) 2,500 monthly credits (up to 500 songs), priority queue. Full commercial ownership rights for monetizeable content.
Premier Plan ($30/mo) 10,000 monthly credits (up to 2,000 songs), advanced features. Full commercial rights & studio stem downloads.

How to Write Effective Prompts in Suno AI

Maximizing sound quality in Suno AI depends on structured prompt engineering:

  1. Specify Genre & Instrumentation: Combine core musical styles (e.g., "Indie Folk, acoustic guitar, warm cello, soft percussion") rather than generic descriptors.
  2. Define Mood & Tempo: Include emotional tone and rhythmic speed (e.g., "melancholic, 90 BPM, atmospheric reverb").
  3. Use Metatags in Custom Lyrics: Structure your custom lyrics with structural bracket tags like [Verse], [Chorus], [Guitar Solo], and [Outro] to guide the AI music arrangement.

Pro Tip: Commercial Rights Ownership

If you generate tracks on Suno AI's paid Pro or Premier plans, you own full commercial rights to monetize your songs on Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, and commercial video games. Songs created on the free tier remain property of Suno and cannot be monetized.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is Suno AI free to use?

Yes. Suno AI provides 50 free credits every day, allowing users to generate up to 10 full songs daily for non-commercial use.

Can I upload my songs to Spotify and YouTube?

Yes, provided you created the songs while subscribed to a paid Pro or Premier plan. Free tier tracks cannot be commercialized.

What is the maximum song length in Suno AI?

With the v3.5 engine, Suno AI generates initial clips up to 4 minutes long, which can then be extended infinitely using the "Extend Track" feature.

Does Suno AI support custom lyrics in foreign languages?

Yes. Suno AI supports multilingual text generation and custom lyric input in over 50 languages, including English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, and Urdu.
